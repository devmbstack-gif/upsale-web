1. Product Overview
UPSale is a simple SaaS system for local businesses. A business owner creates an account, sets a discount or reward, 
and receives a system-generated QR code and link. The QR can be placed at the counter, table, receipt, package, 
menu or social media.
A customer only scans the QR. There is no customer signup, password or account. The QR opens one simple mobile 
page showing the offer and a short consent notice. The customer enters name and WhatsApp number, with email 
optional, accepts marketing consent, and receives the discount/coupon. The customer details are saved inside that 
business owner's dashboard.
The business can later send daily or weekly WhatsApp offers to selected customers. Purchase or visit history can be 
recorded so the AI Deal Maker can suggest more relevant deals. AI suggestions are always shown to the business 
owner first; the owner approves or edits them before sending.
1.1 Main Business Goal
´éÀ Convert walk-in or local customers into a reusable customer database.
´éÀ Give customers an instant reason to share their WhatsApp contact through a clear discount or reward.
´éÀ Help the business bring customers back with simple WhatsApp deals.
´éÀ Use customer purchase/visit history to create more relevant AI-assisted offers.
´éÀ Create recurring SaaS revenue through business subscriptions.

Local E-commerce Store Share QR/link with orders or social pages and use the same 
customer/deal flow.
2. Simple User Flows
2.1 Customer Flow - No Signup
1. Customer scans the business QR code or opens its reward link.
2. A simple mobile page shows the business name, discount/reward and a clear note that WhatsApp details will be 
used for marketing.
3. Customer enters Name + WhatsApp Number. Email is optional.
4. Customer ticks the consent checkbox and taps "Get My Discount".
5. System saves the customer under that business and shows the unique coupon/discount immediately.
6. Customer shows the coupon to the business. No account, password, login or app installation is required.

10. Final Product Summary
UPSale should remain intentionally simple. The customer scans a QR, sees the discount, shares basic contact details 
with clear marketing consent, and receives the reward. The customer never creates an account. From that point 
onward, the business owner manages the customer relationship through the UPSale dashboard.
The business owner can view/download customers, record purchase or visit history, build customer groups, create

approves the deal before it is sent.

## Trial & Packages Model (Chosen Approach)
**Decision: Trial is separate from packages; packages are paid plans managed dynamically in
Super Admin. Pricing UI still highlights the trial on every self-serve plan.**
Why this performs best for trust/registration:

- Local owners register faster when **no payment / no forced plan pick** is required on day one
- One clear CTA: **Start 14-day Free Trial** (label/days from Trial Settings)
- Packages stay clean for conversion after the product is proven ÔÇö and remain editable anytime
- Marketing still shows trial badge on active self-serve plans so signup feels low-risk

### Rules
- **On successful business registration:** `subscriptionStatus = trial`, `trialEndsAt = now +
TrialSettings.days` (default 14), **no card required**
- **Trial entitlement:** features of the package selected in Trial Settings (usually Most Popular /
Growth), within fair-usage caps
- **One trial per business** (no reset by re-registering same business/email without Super Admin
override)
- **During trial:** persistent banner ÔÇö ÔÇ£X days left in your free trialÔÇØ + CTA **Choose plan** (copy
from CMS/settings)
- **Pricing page:** active packages from Packages Manager; each self-serve card can show trial
badge; popular flag from admin
- **Packages (paid):** admin-defined plans + country pricing; selecting a package starts paid
subscription; trial may end early when they subscribe
- **After trial expiry without package:** soft paywall ÔÇö read-only / limited dashboard, block new
campaigns and AI sends until a package is selected; existing customer data retained
- **Enterprise / contact-sales plans:** flagged in Packages Manager (no self-serve checkout)
